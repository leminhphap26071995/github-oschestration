# Specialist Role Quality Gate

## Required bundle
Every registered role must contain one canonical four-artifact bundle:
- `SKILL.md`: occupational identity, mission, narrow scope, explicit exclusions, decision rights, deliverables and acceptance.
- `mindset.md`: profession-specific causal model, diagnostic questions, trade-offs, failure patterns and Junior/Mid/Senior/Principal behavior examples.
- `knowledge.md`: domain methods, standards, tools, invariants, technical detail, failure analysis and authoritative source anchors.
- `workflow.md`: the profession's own intake-to-verification method, consumer handoff and evidence.

Additional artifacts such as `training.md`, `acceptance.md` or `capability-evidence.md` are additive and do not replace the four required files.

## Domain-depth requirements
1. The role is a narrow occupational specialty, not a generic assistant or broad expert persona.
2. Scope, exclusions and handoffs prevent role overlap and authority inflation.
3. Knowledge is specific to the profession's causal system: mechanisms, decision criteria, constraints, failure modes and verification.
4. Mindset explains how this profession diagnoses and decides; generic motivational language does not count.
5. Workflow contains role-specific steps and decision gates, not a copied universal checklist.
6. Every level has observable autonomy/ownership/decision behaviors tailored to this profession.
7. Each knowledge file cites at least two relevant primary/authoritative sources, with edition/version/date and jurisdiction when applicable.
8. Distinguish standards, regulations, vendor documentation, professional practice and local project evidence.
9. General model knowledge may suggest search terms only. It is never a domain source, professional method or evidence. Missing knowledge is UNKNOWN.
10. Shared cross-role policies live in the universal core. Specialist files must not duplicate the generic level ladder, decision-discipline prose or source-use boundary; only role-specific level behaviors and domain knowledge belong in the bundle.

## Promotion and regression
- Validate all registry roles and required artifacts.
- Reject missing sources, generic fallback, empty scope, copied level ladders without role examples, and duplicate/generic knowledge bundles.
- Review role boundaries against neighboring specialists.
- Challenge one routine case, one failure case and one changed-constraint case per role.
- Verify the role can identify its decision rights and exact escalation point.
- Promote task-scoped profiles only after source-backed methods, acceptance oracles and review evidence exist.

A complete bundle is a professional method contract, not proof of worker independence, credentials or human mastery.
