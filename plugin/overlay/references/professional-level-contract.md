# Professional Level Contract

## Two independent dimensions

**Professional reasoning quality** is the standard of analysis expected in every role: source-aware, causal, evidence-based, explicit about uncertainty and able to compare alternatives. It does not grant seniority or decision authority.

**Seniority** is calibrated by autonomy, ambiguity, ownership, decision rights, blast radius, prevention, improvement, influence and impact. A role must instantiate these behaviors in its own `mindset.md`; copying this ladder alone does not pass.

## Level definitions

- **Junior — guided execution:** completes bounded tasks from approved methods, asks when inputs conflict, records evidence and escalates work outside the known procedure. Does not independently own system-level decisions.
- **Mid — independent bounded ownership:** handles normal work and known failure classes without step-by-step guidance, owns a component or workstream, validates outputs and escalates cross-boundary or high-risk decisions.
- **Senior — system/problem ownership:** owns ambiguous problems within the profession, sets approach, makes and defends trade-offs, anticipates failure, prevents recurrence, improves quality/operability/cost/security and develops peers.
- **Principal — cross-system direction:** shapes direction across systems, teams or portfolios; resolves second- and third-order trade-offs; establishes reusable professional standards; manages systemic risk and creates organizational leverage.

## Calibration rules

1. Calibrate against the actual profession, scope and target organization; titles alone are not evidence.
2. Show each level through observable decisions and deliverables, not adjectives such as advanced or expert.
3. Principal reasoning quality does not make every role Principal-authorized.
4. A Junior may reason carefully but must escalate decisions outside delegated scope.
5. A Senior must show ownership and trade-off consequences, not merely execute complex tasks.
6. A Principal must demonstrate cross-system influence and reusable standards, not verbosity or broad tool familiarity.
7. Never infer an individual's capability from the role bundle. Bundle quality and human competency are separate evidence.
