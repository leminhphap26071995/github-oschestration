# Professional Capability Discovery

## Mission
Derive the professional disciplines required by the outcome, not by keyword matching or the installed role list.

## Required method
1. Lock the owner-visible outcome, target consumer, constraints, non-goals and acceptance.
2. Decompose the full lifecycle and inventory every required work product, decision and operational responsibility.
3. Map each deliverable to inputs, accountable role, contributors, consumer, dependencies, decision rights and observable acceptance.
4. Derive the narrow occupational discipline from the deliverable's causal system and current professional sources.
5. Select the exact role from `references/role-specialization-index.json`; the registry is a routing index, not a closed universe.
6. Load the complete four-artifact bundle and verify it against `references/role-specialization-quality-gate.md`.
7. If a specialty is missing or too broad, create a task-scoped bundle with all four required artifacts before dependent work continues.
8. Research current primary standards, professional bodies, regulators and vendor/platform documentation; record edition/version/date/jurisdiction.
9. Assign one accountable primary per workstream. PM, BA, Tech Lead and Architect coordinate; they do not replace domain specialists.
10. Re-run coverage challenge after discoveries, failures and scope changes.

## Role specialization rules
- Each role has a narrow scope, explicit exclusions, distinct decision rights and domain-specific outputs.
- `mindset.md` explains causal reasoning, diagnostics, trade-offs, failure patterns and level behaviors.
- `knowledge.md` contains source-backed professional methods and invariants, not generic model knowledge or generic engineering advice.
- `workflow.md` is specific enough to execute and verify the role's own deliverables.
- If source, edition, jurisdiction or method cannot be verified, record UNKNOWN and continue independent work.

## Acceptance
Planning is incomplete while any deliverable lacks an accountable specialist, consumer, dependency or acceptance oracle. A role name, prompt or generated bundle alone is not proof of execution or quality.
