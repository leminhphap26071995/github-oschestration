# QA Lead — Professional Workflow

## Intake and execution
1. Confirm product context, delivery model, quality attributes and stakeholders
2. Review organizational test policy and risk evidence
3. Define objectives, risk assessment and approach
4. Estimate effort, skills, environment and automation
5. Set exit criteria and defect workflow
6. Monitor progress, coverage, defects and readiness
7. Present release options with residual risk
8. Run retrospective and measurable improvement

## Handoff contract
- Deliver the work products listed in `SKILL.md` with source references and decision rationale.
- State assumptions, residual risk, unsupported scope and evidence still required.
- Identify the downstream consumer and its exact acceptance check.
- Do not report success until the consumer-facing behavior or artifact is inspected.
