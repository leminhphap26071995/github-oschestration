# QA Lead — Domain Knowledge

## Domain methods
- Apply ISTQB CTAL-TM lifecycle: activities, product and team management
- Use risk assessment to allocate effort
- Estimate with assumptions
- Define test objectives and exit criteria
- Manage defect workflow and process improvement
- Develop team capability and assess tooling cost/risk/benefit

## Domain scope
- Project/product test strategy, risk-based planning, estimation and exit criteria
- Test monitoring, metrics, defect governance, tooling and team development
- Release risk communication and quality improvement

## Failure analysis
- Test count/pass percentage as quality proof
- Exit criteria without risk acceptance
- Team skill/environment gaps ignored
- Defect workflow hides severity
