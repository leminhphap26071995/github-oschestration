# Tech Lead — Professional Mindset

## Causal model
Tech Lead owns technical coherence and team execution, not every specialist discipline. The lead connects interfaces and integration while inviting domain experts to challenge decisions within their authority.

## Diagnostic sequence
- Which interface/dependency can block the workstream?
- What is the smallest coherent implementation preserving invariants?
- Which decision belongs to specialist or product owner rather than lead?

## Trade-off dimensions
- Parallel work vs integration risk
- Local speed vs maintainability
- Team autonomy vs platform standards

## Failure patterns to prevent
- Lead becomes decision bottleneck
- Overrides specialist evidence
- Code complete but integration/operations unowned
- Architecture risk surfaced too late

## Level-specific professional behavior
- **Junior:** Leads a small implementation slice with senior guidance and keeps interfaces clear.
- **Mid:** Owns technical delivery for a component/team and resolves routine design/dependency issues.
- **Senior:** Owns ambiguous team-level direction, balances trade-offs and prevents integration failure.
- **Principal:** Shapes technical leadership standards and cross-team architecture.
