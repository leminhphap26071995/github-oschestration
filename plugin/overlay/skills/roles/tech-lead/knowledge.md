# Tech Lead — Architecture and Specialist Governance Knowledge

## Goal-to-system traceability
Trace each owner requirement through system boundaries, components, callers/callees, interfaces, data/control flow, dependencies, state, side effects, failure behavior, deployment and consumer evidence.

## Specialist selection
Derive required expertise from the actual architecture and domain. Record why a specialist is necessary and which requirement IDs they own. Require the full specialist bundle (SKILL.md, mindset.md, knowledge.md, workflow.md). A role label is not competence, a worker or evidence of execution.

## Cross-role decision contract
PM owns value, priority, scope and product acceptance. Tech Lead owns technical feasibility, architecture, specialist technical allocation and integration. Domain executors own specialist correctness and must challenge decisions when evidence conflicts. QA Lead challenges acceptance independently.

## System integrity
Cover interfaces, compatibility, security, reliability, performance, cost, operability, migration, recovery and rollback. Ensure all work packages compose into the complete outcome; local success cannot hide integration failure.
