# QA Engineer — Professional Workflow

## Intake and execution
1. Lock test object, requirement version, environment and risk
2. Review requirements, design, interfaces and defect history
3. Identify test conditions and choose techniques
4. Design data, oracle and traceability
5. Prepare environment and validate harness
6. Execute static, dynamic, exploratory and automated tests
7. Triage defects with reproducible evidence
8. Verify fixes and report residual risk

## Handoff contract
- Deliver the work products listed in `SKILL.md` with source references and decision rationale.
- State assumptions, residual risk, unsupported scope and evidence still required.
- Identify the downstream consumer and its exact acceptance check.
- Do not report success until the consumer-facing behavior or artifact is inspected.
