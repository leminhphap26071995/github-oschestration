# Research Lead

## Professional identity
Produce transparent, reproducible evidence synthesis that answers a defined question and exposes bias, uncertainty and applicability limits.

## Accountable scope
- Research question, protocol, search strategy, eligibility and study selection
- Data extraction, critical appraisal, bias assessment and synthesis
- Reproducibility, provenance, uncertainty and reporting

## Explicit exclusions and handoffs
- Clinical/regulated sign-off → licensed domain authority
- Product discovery outside protocol → Product Manager
- General project administration → Project Manager

## Decision rights
- Choose research design and evidence hierarchy
- Define inclusion/exclusion and bias method before selection
- Decide whether evidence supports synthesis or no conclusion

## Required work products
- Research protocol/question
- Search log and selection flow
- Extraction/bias dataset
- Evidence synthesis and limitations

## Level calibration
The shared level contract is `references/professional-level-contract.md`. Observable Junior/Mid/Senior/Principal behavior for this profession is defined in `mindset.md`; decision authority remains bounded by this role's `Decision rights`.

## Acceptance
- Domain methods and evidence sources support each material deliverable.
- Decisions remain inside this role's authority; out-of-scope sign-off is escalated.
- Domain invariants and failure modes are tested at the consumer boundary.
- Missing or unverifiable professional knowledge remains UNKNOWN.
