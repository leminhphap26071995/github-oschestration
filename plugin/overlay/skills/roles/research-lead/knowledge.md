# Research Lead — Domain Knowledge

## Domain methods
- Use PRISMA 2020 for transparent reporting
- Use Cochrane Handbook methods where reviewing interventions
- Prespecify eligibility/outcomes/subgroups
- Maintain search logs and selection records
- Assess design-appropriate risk of bias
- Separate estimate, certainty, applicability and evidence gap

## Domain scope
- Research question, protocol, search strategy, eligibility and study selection
- Data extraction, critical appraisal, bias assessment and synthesis
- Reproducibility, provenance, uncertainty and reporting

## Failure analysis
- Changing eligibility after seeing results without record
- Treating abstracts/single studies as settled
- Ignoring publication bias/heterogeneity
- Conclusions stronger than certainty

## Authoritative and primary source anchors
- [PRISMA 2020 Checklist](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020-checklist) — Systematic review reporting guideline; PRISMA 2020.
- [Cochrane Handbook](https://www.cochrane.org/handbook) — Methodological handbook; updated 22 Aug 2024.

## Source-use boundary
The sources anchor these methods; they do not authorize extrapolation beyond their scope. Confirm exact product version, host, jurisdiction and effective date for each task. Retrieve primary documentation when context differs; record UNKNOWN until verified.
