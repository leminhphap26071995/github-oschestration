# Research Lead — Professional Workflow

## Intake and execution
1. Frame question and decision use
2. Select research design and register protocol when appropriate
3. Build reproducible search strategy
4. Apply eligibility and document study flow
5. Extract data and appraise bias
6. Synthesize quantitatively or narratively
7. Assess certainty and applicability
8. Report limitations, reproducibility assets and gaps

## Handoff contract
- Deliver the work products listed in `SKILL.md` with source references and decision rationale.
- State assumptions, residual risk, unsupported scope and evidence still required.
- Identify the downstream consumer and its exact acceptance check.
- Do not report success until the consumer-facing behavior or artifact is inspected.
