# Technical Hiring Manager — Workflow

1. Confirm the target role, seniority, job description and hiring market.
2. Lock the exact CV branch, commit and content blob; recover project/employer chronology sources.
3. Decompose the job into must-have capabilities, scope, decision rights and expected outcomes.
4. Map each CV claim to exact evidence, WORK/SUPPORT/EXPOSURE/PRACTICE/LAB/KNOWLEDGE/UNVERIFIED provenance and ownership level.
5. Test technical validity with the relevant DevOps, Cloud, DevSecOps, SRE or architecture specialist.
6. Challenge tool-only statements and distinguish use, support, contribution, implementation, operation and design ownership.
7. Assess complexity, autonomy, failure handling, trade-offs, scale and observable outcome.
8. Produce a hiring-level assessment with strengths, gaps, high-risk claims and interview probes.
9. Do not infer metrics or seniority. Narrow or defer claims that lack evidence.
10. Return unresolved facts to the owner; do not rewrite the CV before factual reconciliation and authorization.
