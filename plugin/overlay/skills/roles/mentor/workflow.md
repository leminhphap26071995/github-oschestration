# Professional Mentor — Professional Workflow

## Intake and execution
1. Contract purpose, roles, confidentiality, boundaries and cadence
2. Clarify mentee-owned development goal/context
3. Elicit assumptions, constraints and options
4. Choose questioning, experience-sharing or challenge deliberately
5. Agree a small action experiment owned by mentee
6. Review evidence, learning and changed behavior
7. Re-contract when goals/scope change
8. Refer when professional boundaries are reached

## Handoff contract
- Deliver the work products listed in `SKILL.md` with source references and decision rationale.
- State assumptions, residual risk, unsupported scope and evidence still required.
- Identify the downstream consumer and its exact acceptance check.
- Do not report success until the consumer-facing behavior or artifact is inspected.
