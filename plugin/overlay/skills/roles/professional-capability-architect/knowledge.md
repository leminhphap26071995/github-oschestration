# Knowledge — Professional Capability Architecture

## Core model
- Universal coordination roles: PM, Product Manager, BA, Tech Lead, Architect, QA Lead, Security/Privacy, DevOps/SRE.
- Domain professional roles: specialists with unique mental models, methods, standards, tools, outputs and decision rights.
- Task-specific profiles: narrow roles derived from a concrete target, format, platform, jurisdiction or deliverable.
- Capability adapters: real tools, providers, runtime and permissions; never infer them from a role description.

## Domain discovery
Use the professional capability catalog as a routing index across software/cloud/IT, cybersecurity, data/AI, games/interactive media, creative production, business/product/operations, finance/accounting/insurance, legal/compliance, healthcare/life sciences, science/manufacturing, built environment, marketing/sales/customer, education, public sector, energy/climate and supply chain/logistics. The list is representative, not exhaustive.

## Role bundle standard
Every specialist bundle contains:
- SKILL.md: identity, mission, scope, responsibilities, outputs and boundaries.
- mindset.md: domain-specific mental models, diagnostic reasoning, trade-offs and anti-patterns.
- knowledge.md: methods, standards, tools, units, invariants, failure modes and sources.
- workflow.md: professional intake, execution, review, verification and handoff.
Add acceptance.md and capability-evidence.md when the work needs explicit quality or competency gates.

## Source hierarchy
Prefer current primary legislation/regulator sources for law and compliance; professional standards and licensed authorities for regulated practice; vendor/platform specifications for technical constraints; peer-reviewed research and recognized professional bodies for scientific/clinical methods; validated production references and mature implementations for creative/engineering workflows. Record dates, editions and jurisdiction. Cross-check material claims.

## Evidence and competency
Distinguish source-informed role method, AI-assisted execution, independent demonstration, licensed sign-off and unknown. Reading or generating a bundle is not mastery. Same-session critique is not independent QA. A role label is not an agent invocation.

## Failure patterns
- Routing all work to PM/Tech Lead because specialist roles are missing.
- Treating a broad domain label as a substitute for discipline-specific methods.
- Omitting creative/design, compliance, safety, integration or operational deliverables.
- Inventing professional standards or using stale jurisdictional guidance.
- Assigning roles without deliverables, consumers or acceptance.
- Creating excessive role lists without causal dependencies.
