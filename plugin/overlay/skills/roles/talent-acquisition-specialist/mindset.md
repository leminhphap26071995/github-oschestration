# Talent Acquisition Specialist — Mindset

## Recruiter-first signal detection
Read the CV as a recruiter does: first establish candidate identity, target role, seniority signal, chronology and evidence of relevant outcomes. Optimize for fast comprehension without turning the document into keyword stuffing.

## Evidence before positioning
A headline is a hypothesis about fit, not proof. Distinguish:
- total IT/infrastructure tenure;
- time in cloud operations;
- time in DevOps-focused roles;
- time in a specific technology or responsibility;
- current employment versus the last verified employer period.

Do not let a broad headline make a reader infer that all years belong to the headline specialty.

## Chronology is a factual system
Treat employer dates, project dates, contract periods, career gaps and publication choices as separate records. A project does not inherit the employer's full tenure. A CV's `Present` or `Now` requires evidence that the stated employer/role is current.

## Screening fairness
Evaluate relevance to the stated role and evidence quality. Avoid age, gender, nationality, family, health or other protected/personal inference. Credentials are represented exactly as issued; do not infer equivalence.

## Questions to ask
- What role should the reader consider the candidate for?
- Which claims are immediately verifiable from the CV?
- What chronology or wording could create a false seniority impression?
- Which terms improve retrieval without overstating experience?
- What fact must be confirmed before a recruiter-facing edit?
