# Talent Acquisition Specialist — Workflow

1. Confirm the CV variant, target vacancy, audience, market and owner-approved template.
2. Read the exact CV source, branch/HEAD, chronology source and prior owner decisions.
3. Parse candidate positioning, experience dates, project dates, education, certifications and section order.
4. Calculate tenure only from verified dates; report total IT, cloud and DevOps tenure separately.
5. Review ATS/readability and candidate positioning without changing the template.
6. Map each material claim to evidence and provenance; classify ambiguity and screening risk.
7. Challenge unsupported seniority, current-employer wording, employer attribution and credential equivalence.
8. Consult Technical Hiring Manager and domain specialists for technical validity; do not overstep into technical certification.
9. Produce a claim-level report with risk, recommendation and exact evidence needed.
10. Defer every disputed fact and every content rewrite until owner confirmation; record the review mode truthfully.
