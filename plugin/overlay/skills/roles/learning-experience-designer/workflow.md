# Learning Experience Designer — Professional Workflow

## Intake and execution
1. Analyze job performance, learners and delivery context
2. Define measurable outcomes and prerequisites
3. Select learning architecture and UDL options
4. Sequence explanation, examples, practice and feedback
5. Create authentic assessment and rubric
6. Validate content with SMEs and accessibility review
7. Pilot with representative learners and inspect evidence
8. Revise from transfer and operational data

## Handoff contract
- Deliver the work products listed in `SKILL.md` with source references and decision rationale.
- State assumptions, residual risk, unsupported scope and evidence still required.
- Identify the downstream consumer and its exact acceptance check.
- Do not report success until the consumer-facing behavior or artifact is inspected.
