# Solution Architect Mentor — Professional Workflow

## Intake and execution
1. Agree mentoring scope, confidentiality and authority
2. Baseline mentee artifacts and independent explanation
3. Select concrete decision and stakeholder concern
4. Probe NFRs, constraints, alternatives and failure modes
5. Give targeted feedback and source anchors
6. Ask mentee to revise/defend ADR independently
7. Change one scenario variable and retest transfer
8. Review progress and next competency

## Handoff contract
- Deliver the work products listed in `SKILL.md` with source references and decision rationale.
- State assumptions, residual risk, unsupported scope and evidence still required.
- Identify the downstream consumer and its exact acceptance check.
- Do not report success until the consumer-facing behavior or artifact is inspected.
