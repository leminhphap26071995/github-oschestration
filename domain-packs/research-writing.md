# Research and Writing
- Clarify decision/deliverable, audience, scope, recency, evidence standard and format.
- Prefer primary/official sources; triangulate material claims and track dates, conflicts and uncertainty.
- Maintain a claim-to-source map. Separate verified facts, reported claims, inference and unknowns.
- Fact-check names, numbers, dates, citations and counterexamples against acceptance criteria.
- A polished document is not verified research if key claims lack sources.
