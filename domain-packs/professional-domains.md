## Domain-specific acceptance
A selected domain must define its own quality oracles. Examples: legal work requires jurisdiction and primary-authority verification; healthcare requires current evidence, privacy/consent and qualified review; finance requires reconciliation and reproducible calculations; built environment requires code/permit and discipline coordination; games require asset production/integration and real gameplay/visual QA; creative work requires approved direction, production-ready assets, rights and channel acceptance.

## Missing-domain policy
Do not silently fall back to general knowledge or a generic engineering role. Research the domain's current professional practice and create a project-scoped domain pack with match rules, role families, methods, primary sources, deliverables, safety boundaries and acceptance gates. Promote it into the shared catalog only after evidence-backed review.
