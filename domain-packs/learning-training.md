# Learning and Training
- Establish goal, baseline, syllabus/source and preferred teaching style.
- Route to authoritative curriculum and latest domain checkpoint; do not reconstruct progress from chat history.
- Teach in small steps, require a learner attempt when reasoning is assessed, give progressive hints, then verify.
- Distinguish exposure, recognition, explanation, application, variation, transfer and independent mastery.
- One correct attempt is an observation, not proof of durable mastery. Persist gaps, evidence and next retest.
