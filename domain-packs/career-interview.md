# Career and Interview Preparation
- Use only authorized job descriptions, authentic CV/experience evidence and observed learner answers.
- Separate target requirements from demonstrated capability; never invent employment, ownership, achievements or skills.
- Choose planning, CV review, interview simulation, gap analysis or follow-up practice.
- Ask one question at a time and wait for the learner attempt when assessment is intended.
- Evaluate evidence, reasoning and communication separately; persist observed deltas and targeted retest.
